What to watchThe nearest cliff is regulatory and fast-moving: the EU AI Act's content-labelling duties turn binding on August 2, with a July 22 deadline to sign the Commission's transparency code, forcing AI-generated streaming creative into visible disclosure within weeks. Watch, too, whether Britain's competition and media regulators wave through Sky's £1.6bn move on ITV — a deal that would concentrate UK premium streaming supply under one owner just as the Sky-ITV-Channel 4 marketplace opens — and whether the FIFA World Cup, now under way, proves out the CTV demand that platforms from ViX to JioHotstar have pre-sold against.
