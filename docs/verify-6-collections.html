Curated reading arcs that thread chapters from several corpora into one continuous argument.

Faith & Doubt

Belief at the edge of reason. The historical Jesus and his forerunner; Jung's God-image transformed in Answer to Job; the Calvinist substrate that outlived its creed; and phenomenology's reach toward the holy. Five corpora, read in sequence, become one argument about what survives when faith meets the modern mind.

5 corpora · 6 chapters · ~3.1 hr

The Medium & the Message

How media remakes the people who make it. McLuhan's probes — the medium as the message — carried forward into the IP address layer that now identifies the watcher. From the global village to the machine that reads your address.

2 corpora · 4 chapters · ~2.2 hr

Heroes & History

Carlyle's lifelong wager that history is the biography of great men — traced across his three monuments. The Calvinist formation of the prophet, his theory of revolution as a sea of fire, and the hero-worship of his vast Friedrich.

3 corpora · 3 chapters · ~76 min
