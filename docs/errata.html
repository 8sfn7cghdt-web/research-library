Corrected; now readsLiveRamp's own blog materials assert that RampID-to-RampID matching delivers 44% higher match rates than HEM-to-HEM matching, but no methodology, sample, publisher list, or time period is disclosed in those materials, and no independent third-party source corroborates the figure.
